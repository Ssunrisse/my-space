Important to know

My Space stores your information in this browser. Backups let you move it to another device or restore it after clearing browser storage.

Close other My Space tabs before restoring. Restoring replaces existing notes and the supported preferences and data in localStorage. After a successful restore, reload the app to see the changes.

Keep backup files private: they may include personal notes and tasks. Backups do not include music or other files from your computer.
